CONTENT CREATION AGREEMENT

Date: 1 September 2026
Parties: Stride Athletics Ltd ("Company") and Jordan Blake ("Influencer").

1. Services. Influencer will create three (3) Instagram Reels featuring Stride running shoes, delivered by 30 September 2026.
2. Fee. Company will pay £800 (GBP) within forty-five (45) days of receiving Influencer's invoice.
3. Usage. Company may use the Reels in paid social advertising in the United Kingdom only, from 1 October 2026 to 31 December 2026.
4. Partnership Ads. Influencer will authorise Company to run the Reels as Instagram Partnership Ads from 1 October 2026 until 31 October 2026.
5. Exclusivity. From 1 October 2026 until 30 November 2026 Influencer will not promote any other athletic footwear or sportswear brand.
6. Revisions. Up to two (2) rounds of revisions per Reel.
7. Cancellation. If Company cancels after the brief is approved, it will pay 50% of the Fee.
